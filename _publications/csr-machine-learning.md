Using machine learning to predict the relationship between ESG scores and corporate financial performance.

## Abstract

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) has long been discussed since the 1950s. This long-debated topic of adopting CSR often draws divided conclusions due to the fact that the reward for investing in CSR practice is often delayed and unquantifiable.

We constructed machine learning models to examine the relationship between ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) scores and the firm's ROE (Return of Equity).

## Models Built

- Support Vector Machine (SVM)
- Random Forest
- Naive Bayesian
- Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) Neural Networks
- Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) Neural Networks

## Key Results

- **MLP model achieved 81.08% accuracy** in predicting the relationship between ESG scores and ROE
- Neural network-based models generally achieved good accuracy
- This work establishes grounds for quantifying the financial benefit CSR can bring to a firm
