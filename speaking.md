## Publications

Lui, G., **Chia, J.**, & Shum, C. (2024). [Impact of Sustainability Disclosures on Financial Performance: A Natural Language Processing Perspective.](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/291ef876-aa37-4fd1-ba86-ac44882cf739/content) *Proceedings of the 57th Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences.*

> This study uses NLP to analyse sustainability reports — examining topics, readability, and sentiment — and their relationship with corporate financial performance for Singapore-listed companies.

Teoh, T. T., Heng, Q. K., **Chia, J. J.**, et al. (2019). [Machine Learning-Based Corporate Social Responsibility Prediction.](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9095846) *IEEE Conference on Robotics, Automation and Mechatronics (RAM).*

> We built ML models (SVM, Random Forest, MLP, LSTM) to predict the relationship between ESG scores and ROE, achieving 81% accuracy with the MLP model.
